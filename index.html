Services

What a smaller business actually needs

Most clients come to us without a digital department. We scope the job to the customers you have now, then leave your team able to run it.

01

Website & web app

A site people can use on a phone, with a clear route to enquire, book or buy. Useful when the current site is hard to update, or it does not bring in the work.

02

Phone & tablet app

An app for the customers or staff who live on a phone. A better fit than a website when the job is repeated every day, such as bookings, updates or a tool for your team.

03

Content system

A back end your office can use. We build it, train the people who will publish, and stay on for maintenance so you are not calling a developer for every change.

How it works

Three steps. No agency fog.

You are running a business while this gets built. Each step has a job, and you always know whether we are listening, building or getting ready to launch.

  1. 1

    Consultation

    We start with your customers

    We look at how people find you, what they need to do, and what is getting in the way. The recommendation is the simplest build that does that job.

    • How a customer moves from first visit to enquiry
    • What the business needs the system to do this year
    • Where a simpler approach will do
    • What is worth fixing before anything new is added
  2. 2

    Development

    We build something your team can live with

    You see the work as it comes together. It works on the phones your customers actually use, and it stays faithful to your brand.

    • Clear on phones, tablets and desktops
    • Built with current HTML, CSS and JavaScript
    • Checked, reusable and backed up
    • Straightforward for the person who will update it
  3. 3

    Launch

    You can run it after we leave

    We test it with you before anyone else sees it. If there is a content system, we train the people who will use it.

    • Checked on the devices your customers use
    • Nothing goes live until you are happy
    • Training so your team can publish
    • A month of free defect fixes, then support if you want it
